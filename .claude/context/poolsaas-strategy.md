# PoolSaaS Project Memory & Strategy Guide

## Core Thesis & Mindset

- **The Bottleneck:** Technical capacity is _not_ the bottleneck; market exposure and distribution reps are.
- **The Anti-Pattern:** Avoid the "Builder's Comfort Zone." Do not write code, manage automated agentic workflows, or prematurely optimize an engineering stack before validation.
- **The Goal:** Collect clear market validation signals and commitment reps with minimal time investment, balancing entrepreneurial goals alongside a software engineering management career and family.

---

## The Existing Asset (The Audubon Core)

- **Current Status:** Live, production-tested B2B utility running at the Audubon, IA community pool.
- **Current Price point:** $50/month (paid via physical check to avoid processing friction and align with city accounting requirements).
- **Core Value Proposition:** Acts as an operational audit trail. Solves accountability for teenage lifeguard shifts, concessions tracking, and gate check-ins, replacing messy paper logs and cash drawer discrepancies.

---

## The Target Market & Seasonal Play

- **The Window:** Late July through September (The Post-Summer Retrospective). This is the optimal window to reach out while seasonal operational pain is fresh but daily manager fatigue is winding down.
- **The Municipal Budget Cycle (Iowa):**
  - **July 1 – June 30:** Fiscal Year.
  - **August – November:** Operational review and internal departmental wishlist building.
  - **January – February:** City Council budget workshops.
  - **April 30:** Legal certification deadline for city budgets.
  - _Strategy:_ Target them during the wishlist phase so a $2,400/year utility can slide directly into department-level discretionary spending caps ($2,500–$5,000 threshold) without forcing formal council approval or competitive bidding.

### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) By Town Size

1. **Tiny Communities (< 2,500 pop):** Target the **City Clerk** directly (they manage the macro-budget and absorb administrative billing friction).
2. **Mid-Sized Regional Hubs (> 2,500 pop):** Target the **Director of Parks and Recreation** (full-time, year-round decision-makers looking for off-season operational improvements). Avoid seasonal summer pool managers who lack purchasing authority.

---

## High-Leverage Offer & Pricing Strategy

- **The Positioning:** Move from "hobbyist" pricing ($50/mo) to professional B2B SaaS pricing (**$199/month** structured as a $2,400 annual license billed in 12 monthly installments).
- **The Justification for Annual Billing:** Ensures off-season data continuity for municipal audits, winter reporting, and early spring preseason setup. Avoids manual proration administration.
- **The "Founding Partner" Offer:**
  - Leverage identity as a local independent Iowa developer.
  - Frame the current baseline as a proven system running in Audubon.
  - Invite 3 regional hubs to be "Founding Partners" where custom feature requirements (e.g., pool party tracking, complex scheduling gaps) are co-developed over the winter off-season.
  - Zero upfront development costs in exchange for a committed annual contract. Only write code _after_ a contract or letter of intent is signed.
